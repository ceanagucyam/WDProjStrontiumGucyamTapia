History of NewJeans

NewJeans is a South Korean girl group formed by ADOR under HYBE. They debuted on July 22, 2022, and quickly became one of the fastest-rising K‑pop groups of the 4th generation. Known for their fresh Y2K-inspired music, minimalist concepts, and trending hits like “Attention,” “Hype Boy,” “OMG,” and “Super Shy,” NewJeans gained worldwide popularity in record time.
